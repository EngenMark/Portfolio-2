My contribution to the RV60 project – Vision Inspection System

My task in the RV60 project was to substantially improve an existing machine for visual inspection of bottles and jars. The previous design had several limitations that made everyday operation difficult:

  • The upper cover opened sideways along the conveyor. Any additional component, such as a sensor, could obstruct it and restrict access to the cameras and lighting.
  • The operator panel with the PLC screen was mounted on one side only, limiting operating ergonomics.
  • Defective bottles were pushed onto a side conveyor by a pneumatic cylinder. The conveyor filled up quickly and caused downtime.

For the new version, I designed:

  • an upward-opening front cover with a monitor and two rear doors for full maintenance access,
  • an operator panel on a swivelling support, allowing operation from either side,
  • an integrated rotary accumulation table for rejected products, eliminating downtime caused by the previous conveyor filling up,
  • a “piano” mechanism with 8 independent fingers that push bottles out sequentially, allowing individual containers to be rejected even with a spacing of just 3 cm.

These changes turned RV60 into a modern, ergonomic and reliable inspection machine, ready for demanding production conditions.
