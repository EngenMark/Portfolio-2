My contribution to the RC50 project – ROPP Capping Machine

The company I worked for was developing a complete production line. I was fully responsible for designing the RC50 machine, from the concept and mechanical design through to the technical documentation.

The original plan called for two separate machines: one station for applying ROPP caps and another for crimping atomizers. I combined both functions in a single machine, saving space and simplifying integration into the line.

My design solutions reduced changeover time from 20 minutes to less than 5 minutes, with only one operator required instead of two.

I designed the entire machine from scratch. My work included:

  • ROPP capping spindles,
  • a system for adjusting the pressing force,
  • a support column for the customer-supplied cap sorter, designed to suit its specifications,
  • the complete pneumatic system, including valve manifolds, cylinders and pneumatic clamps.

This was the company’s first project of this type. RC50 represents an important engineering achievement and demonstrates my ability to design advanced special-purpose machinery.
