My contribution to the UO60 project – Inkjet Rotator

My task was to design a versatile machine for inkjet printing on different types of containers, from PET bottles to glass jars. The main objective was a simple, compact design that could be integrated easily into different production lines.

For the UO60 project, I designed the complete machine, including:

  • a mechanism that positions containers according to a previously scanned barcode,
  • a servo-driven rotary module for precise print positioning,
  • a system that guides and stabilizes containers during rotation and marking.

UO60 is an example of a compact and carefully considered engineering solution.
