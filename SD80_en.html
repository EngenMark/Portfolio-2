SD80 – Sticker Dispenser

Renders • Engineering solutions

My contribution to the project SD80 – Sticker Dispenser

I designed SD80 from scratch as a machine for sealing product cartons, developing both the machine concept and its mechanical design.

  • Automatic adjustment to the carton dimensions.
  • Application and folding of sealing stickers at the top and bottom of the carton.
  • Inspection to verify the presence of the seals after application.
  • Automatic ejection of cartons with missing seals from the machine.
